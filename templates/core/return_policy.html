Return & Refund Policy

At Book Nest, we hold our literary craftsmanship to the highest standards. Here is our comprehensive, transparent return policy.

Return Time Limit

You have up to 14 calendar days from the date of physical delivery to initiate a return or exchange request for any eligible book purchased via Book Nest.

Eligible Conditions for Return

  • The book is unread, undamaged, and in its original, pristine collector state.
  • Original protective packaging, bookmarks, or dust jackets are intact.
  • The item received arrived with manufacturing defects, missing pages, or shipping damage.
  • The book delivered differed from the edition or title ordered.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Digital eBooks or downloadable audio materials once downloaded.
  • Personalized / author-inscribed special collector copies.
  • Books with visible signs of spine cracking, highlighter markings, or dog-eared pages caused after receipt.
  • Items marked on final clearance sale.

Refund Policy & Methods

Approved refunds will be credited directly to your original method of payment (or via bank transfer for Cash on Delivery orders) within 5–7 business days following our warehouse inspection.

Return & Exchange Process

  1. Reach out to our support at curators@aditibohara.com.np with your Order Number (#BN-XXXX) and photos if damaged.
  2. Our concierge will email you a prepaid return shipping slip within 24 hours.
  3. Pack the book safely in the original protective box and drop it at your nearest logistics partner.
  4. Upon receipt and verification, your replacement or refund is promptly released.

Questions regarding your order?

Contact our dedicated team directly via our Contact Page or email curators@aditibohara.com.np.
