Privacy & Trust

Privacy Policy & Security Standards

How Book Nest strictly respects your confidentiality, secures your reading preferences, and guarantees enterprise privacy.

1. Fortune 500 Level Security Layer

Book Nest implements multi-layered encryption protocols (TLS 1.3, CSRF protection, secure cookie handling, and XSS filtering). We ensure all user session interactions and shopping cart transactions adhere to the strictest security baselines.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to fulfill your orders and customize your reading suggestions: name, delivery address, email, contact telephone, and optional AI recommendation questionnaire answers. We never sell or lease customer information to third parties.

3. PWA & VAPID Web Push Notifications

When opting in for push notifications, public key cryptography via VAPID ensures messages regarding book releases or order updates are sent without exposing your personal device identifiers.

4. Cookie Usage & Consent

Cookies are deployed solely to maintain your session basket, favorite books, and authenticated user preferences.
